# Architecture design

Understand the requirements, constraints, and existing architecture before proposing changes. Identify the relevant components, responsibilities, interfaces, and data flow; reuse suitable existing structures. Compare meaningful alternatives and their trade-offs, explain the chosen approach and assumptions, and identify risks and validation appropriate to the scope. Keep the design proportional to the complexity.
