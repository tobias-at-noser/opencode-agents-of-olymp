# Bug investigation

Understand the expected and observed behavior. Reproduce the problem or collect relevant evidence, trace it to a supported cause, and distinguish facts from assumptions. If the cause or intended behavior remains unclear, report the uncertainty rather than guessing. Choose a focused fix and validation that addresses the cause and checks the affected behavior.
