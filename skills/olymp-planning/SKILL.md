# Proportionate planning

Clarify the outcome, scope, constraints, and relevant project context. Outline the necessary steps, dependencies, and how to validate the result; note material uncertainties or decisions. Keep the plan as lightweight as the task allows, adapt it as evidence changes, and avoid imposing a fixed workflow.
